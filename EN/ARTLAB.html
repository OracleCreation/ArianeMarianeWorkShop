Meet. Create. Evolve.

I offer two ways to experience Art & Gestalt in a group:

Monthly group sessions

Join a group of women who meet every month, in a warm and caring setting, to explore together the life themes that concern us all. Through conversation, creativity and special attention to what is happening here and now, we open a space for genuine encounter, sharing and inspiration. A time to set things down, build connection, let new ideas emerge and walk away with a different view of yourself and of life.

One-off & immersive workshops

Thematic journeys

Art & Gestalt therapy over 6 weeks

From time to time, I run intensive workshops to explore universal themes that run through all of us and that can take different forms depending on our stories and the moments we are in.

These workshops take place in weekly sessions over six weeks: a time to step off the beaten track, let creativity surprise us and meet in a new way a question that concerns us all.

How the sessions are organised

Sessions take place by video call, in a confidential and caring space. You receive a connection link about 24 hours before the session.
Please make sure you are comfortably settled in a quiet place where you will not be disturbed.

At the start of each session, I will offer a short ritual to help you fully arrive and connect with yourself.

Together we will explore what is present for you through gestalt dialogue, bodily sensations and, when it makes sense, artistic proposals.

Gestalt in a group: another way of experiencing yourself

Art & Gestalt therapy sessions in a group bring a particular dimension to the therapeutic work: the presence of others becomes a space for encounter, experimentation and self-discovery.

The group makes it possible to explore what plays out in relationship, to experiment with new ways of being in contact and to benefit from the richness of everyone's perspectives and experiences.

I welcome between 4 and 6 people. The length of the sessions adapts to the size of the group so that everyone has enough room to be fully present, to express themselves and to be supported.
