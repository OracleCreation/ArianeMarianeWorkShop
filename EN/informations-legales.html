Bookings are made on request, through direct contact. Payments are made outside the site, according to the terms agreed at the time of booking: the site offers no online payment.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

I want your personal data to stay personal. This page explains what I collect, why, who I share it with, how long I keep it and how to exercise your rights, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, EU regulation 2016/679) and the French Data Protection Act.

Data controller

Ariane Sroka, 15, rue Nationale, 72340 Loir en Vallée, France. Telephone: +33 (0)6 87 81 08 95. Email: hello@arianemariane.fr

What is personal data?

It is any information that can identify you directly or indirectly: name, first name, email address, phone number, IP address…

What data I collect

Browsing the site requires no registration and no personal data from you. I collect data when you contact me, sign up for a meeting or workshop, or begin support:

  • Identification and contact: name, first name, email address, phone number if you give it, and the content of your message.
  • Registration, orders and administration: the workshop or meeting chosen, or the tailor-made creation requested, organisational exchanges, invoicing and payments.
  • Support: what you choose to entrust to me during sessions and workshops may concern your private life, even your health (so-called sensitive data). This information is covered by the confidentiality described in my code of ethics, and is only processed with your explicit consent. Notes relating to support are kept on a password-protected personal computer, with no storage in a cloud service (see “Where is it stored, and for how long?”).
  • Technical data: your IP address and technical connection information, processed by the host to deliver the site (see “Cookies & hosting”).

I invite you not to describe a specific health situation in the contact form: we will talk about it in person.

Why I use it, and on what legal basis

  • Responding to your requests for information and contact: your approach to me (pre-contractual measures) and your consent.
  • Organising and delivering support, meetings and workshops, managing registrations, invoicing: performance of the contract.
  • Keeping my accounts and meeting my tax obligations: legal obligation.
  • Informing you about upcoming workshops if you ask me to: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Ensuring the security of the site and defending my rights in case of dispute: my legitimate interest and my legal obligations.

Where data is essential to process your request, I tell you so; without it, I will not be able to follow up. I do not use it for profiling or for commercial prospecting without your agreement, and I never sell it.

Newsletter

I may offer, here or later, a newsletter to share news about my work and upcoming offers. You would only be subscribed with your agreement, your email address would only be used for that mailing, and every message would include a simple way to unsubscribe at any time.

Who has access

Your data is only accessible to me. It may be passed to the competent authorities on legal request, and go through providers strictly necessary to my activity, who are not allowed to use it for their own purposes and are bound to an equivalent level of security:

  • the site host: GitHub, Inc.;
  • my email provider, which receives your messages;
  • where applicable, the providers needed for an order or a payment (carrier, payment or video-conferencing service), only for what concerns you.

The up-to-date list of these providers can be obtained on request.

Where is it stored, and for how long?

The site is hosted by GitHub, a US company: technical data (notably the IP address) may therefore be processed outside the European Union. GitHub states that it adheres to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and relies, in addition, on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses: these mechanisms provide a legal framework for these transfers. The contact form records nothing on the site: it prepares an email in your own email software, which you send yourself. Your messages and my exchanges with you are then kept in my email account and on my work computer, password-protected. My notes relating to support are kept locally on a password-protected personal computer, with no storage in a cloud service.

  • Contact messages: 3 years at most after the last exchange, then deletion.
  • Registration and support data: for the duration of the relationship, then archived according to the legal limitation periods.
  • Notes relating to support: 3 years at most after the end of the support, then deletion.
  • Accounting records: 10 years, as required by law.
  • Address for workshop information: until you unsubscribe.

When these periods expire, the data is deleted, or archived offline on a secure medium where the law requires it.

How it is protected

I take appropriate technical and organisational measures to preserve the security, integrity and confidentiality of your data: secure connection to the site, restricted access, regular updates, and verification of my providers' guarantees. Access to my support notes is strictly reserved to me, and I maintain suitable precautions to preserve their confidentiality: device security, backups and access management.

Your rights

  • Access to your data, and rectification if it is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • erasure (right to be forgotten), in the cases provided for by law: this is not an absolute right, and I may have to keep certain data for legal or legitimate reasons;
  • restriction of processing and objection to processing;
  • portability: receiving your data in a readable format, or transferring it to another controller;
  • withdrawal of your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before;
  • directives on what happens to your data after your death;
  • complaint to the CNIL (www.cnil.fr) if you believe your rights are not being respected.

To exercise these rights, write to me by email or by post (contact details above); I reply within one month. I may ask you for proof of identity in case of doubt.

Changes to this policy

I may change it at any time. The new version applies as soon as it is published on this page, with the date above updated.
