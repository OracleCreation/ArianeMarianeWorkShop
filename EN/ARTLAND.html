ARTventureLand

Art & Gestalt retreats and workshops in the Loir Valley

In the heart of the beautiful Loir valley, ARTventureLand is an invitation to enjoy a creative and restorative holiday.

Between artistic discoveries, immersive workshops and moments of sharing, dive into an inspiring setting to slow down, experiment and reconnect with your unique creativity.

Through artistic experiences and a Gestalt and psychocorporal approach, you are invited to freely explore colours, materials, sensations and the possibilities of your personal expression.

I support you at your own pace and according to your needs, in a confidential and secure setting, with warmth and kindness.

Let your creativity carry you

ARTventureLand is made for you if you…

  • Feel like creating or want to explore something new.
  • Feel the need to take a pause, reflect and reconnect with yourself.
  • Are looking for answers.
  • Are curious to discover what can emerge when you let go.
  • Want to live the present in the here and now and let yourself be carried away by play!

Individually or in a group

ARTventureLand can be lived solo, with friends or in a group, depending on the experience you are looking for: a time to recharge and be supported, or the richness and stimulation of a shared ARTventure.

ARTventureLand

to live creative, joyful and lively experiences.

Convinced that everyone carries within them an artist who only asks to express themselves, I design each experience tailor-made, taking into account your wishes, your rhythm and your sensitivity.

Here, there is no pursuit of perfection and no expected result. We experiment freely, play with colours, textures and materials, follow our intuition and let ourselves be surprised by what is revealed as we create.

At ARTventureLand, what matters is to fully live the creative process, in the pleasure of making and the freedom to explore.

THE ARTVENTURE BEYOND THE STUDIO

EXPLORE. WANDER. GET INSPIRED.

Nestled in the heart of the Loir Valley, ARTventureLand invites you to extend the experience along the paths, villages and landscapes that surround it. Vineyards, hillsides, forests and tuffeau-stone villages make up a rich and inspiring landscape.

Let the region feed your creativity: stroll, discover, breathe, marvel… and above all, let yourself be surprised!
