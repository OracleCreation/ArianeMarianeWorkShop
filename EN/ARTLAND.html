ARTventureLand

Art & Gestalt as an adventure of the self

In the heart of the beautiful Loir valley, ARTventureLand invites you to a creative and restorative getaway, combining artistic discoveries, immersive workshops and moments of sharing. Step into an inspiring setting to slow down, experiment and reconnect with your creativity, with yourself and with the world around you.

Through intuitive art, artistic experiences and the gestalt approach, you are invited to freely explore colours, materials, sensations and the possibilities of your personal expression.

Let your creativity carry you

ARTventureLand is for you if you…

  • Feel like creating or want to explore something new.
  • Need to take a break, to reflect and to reconnect with yourself.
  • Are looking for answers.
  • Are curious to discover what can emerge when you let go.
  • Want to live in the present, in the here and now, and let yourself be carried away by play!

ARTventureLand for restorative, joyful and lively interludes.

Convinced that everyone carries an artist within who only asks to express themselves, I design each experience to measure, to welcome you where you are, with your wishes, your pace and your sensitivity.
If you feel like creating, exploring and being surprised, ARTventureLand is made for you. Come and experiment freely, play with colours, textures and materials. Follow your intuition and discover what wants to emerge along the way.
At ARTventureLand, we are not looking for perfection but for fully living the process.

And if you have a particular creative wish, a technique you would like to discover or a project you want to explore, don't hesitate to contact me so we can imagine it together.

At ARTventureLand, even the impossible can become possible!

Imagination as the only limit!

ARTVENTURE BEYOND THE STUDIO

EXPLORE. WANDER. BE INSPIRED.

Nestled in the heart of the Loir Valley, ARTventureLand invites you to extend the experience along the paths, villages and landscapes that surround it. Vineyards, hillsides, forests and tuffeau-stone villages make up a rich and inspiring landscape.

Let the land nourish your creativity: wander, discover, breathe, marvel… and above all: let yourself be surprised!
